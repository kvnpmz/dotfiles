-------
Wi-Fi (NetworkManager / nmcli)
-------

* Check status:
  nmcli general status

* Show Wi-Fi networks:
  nmcli device wifi list

* Connect:
  nmcli device wifi connect "SSID" password "PASSWORD"

* Show current connection:
  nmcli connection show --active

* Disconnect:
  nmcli device disconnect wlan0

-------
Bluetooth (bluetoothctl)
-------

* Enter Bluetooth:
  bluetoothctl

Inside bluetoothctl:

* List devices:
  devices

* Scan:
  scan on

* Stop scan:
  scan off

* Pair:
  pair MAC_ADDRESS

* Connect:
  connect MAC_ADDRESS

* Disconnect:
  disconnect MAC_ADDRESS

* Forget device:
  remove MAC_ADDRESS

* Trust device (auto-connect):
  trust MAC_ADDRESS

* Exit:
  quit
